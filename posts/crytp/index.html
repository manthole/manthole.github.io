Exercice 1 :

In this exercice we have to find the flag on this message.

M'DL GRQQH PRQ QRP D FH FKLIIUH, PDLV F'HVW XQH ULJRODGH

To proceed, I seach on the website dcode, and the caesar cipher came out of it.

The solution of this exercice after decryption is :

APPARU CENT ANS AVANT JC, JE SUIS LE PREMIER FLAG
J'AI DONNE MON NOM A CE CHIFFRE, MAIS C'EST UNE RIGOLADE
